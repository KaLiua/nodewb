Why environmental protection matters

Healthy ecosystems provide clean air and water, stabilize the climate, and support human well-being. Everyone — individuals, businesses, and governments — has a role to play.

Climate & Energy

Reduce greenhouse gas emissions by choosing efficient energy, reducing waste, and supporting clean energy policies.

Biodiversity

Protect habitats, support native species, and reduce pollution to help ecosystems thrive.

Waste & Pollution

Cut single-use plastics, recycle properly, and choose non-toxic products.

Practical tips you can start today

  • Reduce energy use: Switch to LED bulbs, unplug chargers, and set a smart thermostat.
  • Choose sustainable transport: Walk, cycle, carpool or use public transit when possible.
  • Minimize single-use items: Carry a reusable bottle, bag, and cutlery.
  • Buy less, choose well: Repair items, buy second-hand, and prefer durable goods.
  • Support nature: Plant native flowers, avoid pesticides, and create habitat in your garden.

Get involved

Small actions add up. Join local cleanups, volunteer for tree planting, or support environmental charities. Advocate for better policies in your community.
